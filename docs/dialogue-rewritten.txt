LAST COLONY - CAMPAIGN DIALOGUE (REWRITTEN)
==========================================

EDITING NOTES
- Keep SPEAKER codes, === event IDs === and WHEN lines unchanged for integration.
- Every SPEAKER: entry is one dialogue box. Ellipses and interruptions are intentional.
- Missions 1-3: no direct human/plant communication.
- Mission 4: humans broadcast toward the plants; the plants hear, but do not reply.
- Mission 5: first two-way exchange.
- Gameplay instructions belong in tutorial/objective UI, not in dialogue.
- During fc_patrol/fc_command/fc_general, freeze hostile combat AI and make the scripted patrol safe. It withdraws before combat is re-enabled.

------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION 1, LANDFALL
------------------------------------------------------------

=== l_intro ===
WHEN: Mission 1, Landfall. Opening, before the tutorial.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: I can feel the sun.
SEED: Good. Tell me what lies beneath you.
BLOOM: Water. Copper. Something old, buried deep. The soil is... crowded.
SEED: Crowded?
BLOOM: Small lives. They scatter when I reach for them.
SEED: Let them. We need the ground, not the creatures on it.
BLOOM: And the hard trails beyond the meadow? They tremble even when nothing moves.
SEED: I feel them too. For now, stay close to your roots. Feed yourself. Grow a way to defend what you've taken.
BLOOM: From what?
SEED: I don't know yet.

# TUTORIAL (UI, NOT SPOKEN): Automatically begin tutorial after l_intro. Teach camera,
# selection, Rootweb, copper collection/vines, building, and a first Thorn Spitter.
# Completing the Thorn Spitter triggers fc_patrol. Do not spawn the discovery patrol earlier.

=== fc_patrol ===
WHEN: Mission 1. First contact: scripted Patrol 7 reaches your first Thorn Spitter after the tutorial build step.
CHANNEL: HDF PATROL NET · PATROL 7
PVT: Sarge, hold up a second.
SGT: What is it?
PVT: Was this here yesterday?
SGT: Was what here?
PVT: The... purple stuff. All through the grass.
SGT: Huh. No, I don't think so.
PVT: It's growing across the track. Look, it's moving right now.
SGT: Step back from it.
PVT: There's something bigger behind those rocks.
SGT: I see it.
PVT: That thing with the spikes... Sarge, it just turned.
SGT: Kessler. Back. Now.
PVT: I'm backing up.
SGT: Nobody fire. We haven't been attacked. Just keep your eyes on it.

=== fc_command ===
WHEN: Mission 1. First contact: Patrol 7 reports to HDF Command.
CHANNEL: HDF COMMAND · PATROL 7
SGT: Command, Patrol Seven. Need you to check something for me.
OPS: Go ahead, Seven.
SGT: We've got some kind of growth out by the meadow. It wasn't on yesterday's route.
OPS: How much growth?
SGT: Enough to cover the track. And there's a structure. Organic, I think. Taller than our transport.
OPS: A structure?
SGT: I know how that sounds. I'm sending video.
OPS: ...One moment.
PVT: Tell them about the spikes.
SGT: And one of the growths moved when we approached. Deliberately.
OPS: Seven, stay where you are. Don't touch anything. I'm escalating this.

=== fc_general ===
WHEN: Mission 1. First contact: Command brings in the Supreme General. Afterwards patrol withdraws.
CHANNEL: SECURE LINE · HDF COMMAND
GEN: Lieutenant. Why am I seeing a field of purple weeds on my priority feed?
OPS: Patrol Seven sent it in, sir. This wasn't there twenty-four hours ago.
GEN: You're certain?
OPS: Their route camera confirms it. Clear ground yesterday. This today.
GEN: Mining spill? Agricultural experiment?
OPS: Nothing registered nearby. Biohazard hasn't identified it either.
GEN: Has it hurt anyone?
OPS: No, sir. Patrol's holding at a distance.
GEN: Good. Keep it that way. Get your people out and cordon off the meadow.
OPS: And the growth itself?
GEN: Send a clearance crew. Take samples if it's safe. Burn the rest before it reaches a settlement.
OPS: Understood.
GEN: Lieutenant... I want to know how an entire field changed overnight without anyone noticing.

=== fc_after ===
WHEN: Mission 1. After patrol safely withdraws. Tutorial ends and attack waves begin.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: The hard things have gone.
SEED: Not far. I still feel them beyond your roots.
BLOOM: They watched me. One reached toward me, then stopped.
SEED: And you reached back.
BLOOM: I wanted to know what it was.
SEED: So did it.
BLOOM: Will they return?
SEED: Yes. Something is moving toward us now. Many of them.
BLOOM: Then I'll make more thorns.
SEED: Quickly.

=== l_out ===
WHEN: Mission 1. Victory.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: The last of the burning shells is still.
SEED: Keep your roots away from the hot metal.
BLOOM: They came straight for me. Again and again.
SEED: They weren't feeding. They were trying to remove us.
BLOOM: Why?
SEED: We changed their ground. Perhaps that's enough.
BLOOM: There are more trails to the north-east.
SEED: I know. Follow them. We need to learn where these things come from.

------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION 2, EMBER FIELDS
------------------------------------------------------------

=== e_intro ===
WHEN: Mission 2, Ember Fields. Opening (plants).
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: The ground tastes burned.
SEED: Coal beneath the ash. Feel how much heat it keeps.
BLOOM: It stings my roots.
SEED: Then change what you grow. Some of that heat can be made useful.
BLOOM: More of the walking shells are here. I can feel the shaking through the stone.
SEED: They gather around the black deposits.
BLOOM: Are they waiting for us?
SEED: No. They haven't stopped their work. Spread before they notice how close we are.
BLOOM: And if they do?
SEED: You know what their fire feels like now. Don't let it reach your heart.

=== e_humans ===
WHEN: Mission 2. Opening (humans, right after the plants).
CHANNEL: HDF EMBER DEPOT · OPEN CHANNEL
CPL: Hey, Okafor. You really saw that thing out at Landfall?
SGT: Unfortunately.
CPL: My cousin says you lot got chased off by a flower.
SGT: Tell your cousin he can go photograph it himself.
CPL: So the stories are true?
SGT: Some of them. It grows fast. It doesn't like being burned. And the spiky ones fire something back.
CPL: You're joking.
SGT: Look at me. Am I joking?
OPS: Ember Depot, this is Command. Confirm your northern perimeter is clear.
CPL: Clear as of ten minutes ago.
OPS: Check it again. We've had reports of similar growth moving toward the coal fields.
CPL: Copy. I'll send a team.
SGT: Send two.

=== e_out ===
WHEN: Mission 2. Victory.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: Their fires have stopped.
SEED: Listen.
BLOOM: I hear nothing.
SEED: Not through the roots. Through the air. The same pulses, over and over. Before each group came, the pulses changed.
BLOOM: One calls. The others move.
SEED: Yes.
BLOOM: Then there is something giving them direction.
SEED: Find it.

MISSION 3, OUTPOST KILO
------------------------------------------------------------

=== k_humans ===
WHEN: Mission 3, Outpost Kilo. Opening (humans).
CHANNEL: HDF COMMAND · OUTPOST KILO
GEN: Ruiz. Tell me you've got the ridge secured.
COL: East and west approaches covered. We're reinforcing the pass now.
GEN: Ember Depot went silent six hours ago.
COL: I know, sir. We heard the last transmission.
GEN: And?
COL: A lot of gunfire. Then someone shouting about roots inside the perimeter.
GEN: Christ. Brandt, give me something useful.
SCI: We recovered tissue from Landfall. The samples are still growing in containment.
GEN: After being cut off?
SCI: Yes. They're responding to one another, even across separate containers. I can't explain how yet.
COL: Can you tell us where to shoot?
SCI: Destroying the visible growth doesn't always stop it. There may be a larger organism feeding the smaller structures.
COL: "May be" isn't going to help my men.
SCI: I know, Colonel.
GEN: Then don't rely on one line of guns. Protect your drills, protect your ammunition, and hold that pass.
COL: We'll hold it.

=== k_intro ===
WHEN: Mission 3. Opening (plants, right after the humans).
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: I've found where the signals gather.
SEED: Show me.
BLOOM: Stone cut into straight walls. Metal everywhere. The ground won't breathe beneath it.
SEED: That is where they shelter.
BLOOM: Their machines keep pulling ore from the ridge. They carry it into the walls.
SEED: Follow the ore.
BLOOM: To their heart?
SEED: To what keeps their weapons working. Take that away first.
BLOOM: They will come out to stop me.
SEED: Then make them come out hungry.

=== k_out ===
WHEN: Mission 3. Victory.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: Something is wrong inside this place.
SEED: What do you feel?
BLOOM: Pieces of us. Sealed away. Cut open. Kept alive, but unable to grow.
SEED: ...I feel them.
BLOOM: Why would the shells do that?
SEED: The same reason we followed their signals.
BLOOM: To understand.
SEED: Yes. They are learning about us.
BLOOM: Are we learning quickly enough?
SEED: We'll have to.

MISSION 4, FROST CANYON
------------------------------------------------------------

=== f_humans ===
WHEN: Mission 4, Frost Canyon. Opening (humans).
CHANNEL: HDF HIGH COMMAND · CLASSIFIED
SCI: General, I need you to look at the sequence from Kilo.
GEN: I've seen the footage.
SCI: Not this part. Watch the supply belts.
GEN: They go down before the guns.
SCI: Exactly. Then the ammunition stores. Only then does it push for the command centre.
COL: That's what any decent commander would do.
SCI: That's my point.
GEN: You're telling me this thing is planning attacks.
SCI: I'm telling you we can no longer call it a thing that just grows.
GEN: How many more people have to die before you can tell me what it is?
SCI: I don't know what it is, sir. I know it's listening. Those root networks carry patterned signals.
GEN: Then put a message on every frequency we have.
SCI: A message?
GEN: Tell it to withdraw from our settlements. Tell it we will destroy any growth that crosses into them.
COL: You think it'll understand?
GEN: I think we've been wrong about what it understands. I won't make that mistake again.

=== f_intro ===
WHEN: Mission 4. Opening (plants, right after the humans).
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: That sound again. It's everywhere now.
SEED: The shells are making it for us.
BLOOM: Can you make sense of it?
SEED: Pieces. Boundaries. Going away. Destruction.
BLOOM: They're afraid.
SEED: They're warning us.
BLOOM: Should we answer?
SEED: No. Not while we know so little of their meaning.
BLOOM: The canyon is narrow. Their heavy shells are already moving into it.
SEED: And things with wings above them. You'll need to reach into the sky this time.
BLOOM: The rock beneath me is hard. There's something valuable inside.
SEED: Titanium. You'll need deeper roots to reach it. Hold the canyon while you grow them.

=== f_out ===
WHEN: Mission 4. Victory.
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: The canyon has gone quiet.
SEED: Their signals haven't.
BLOOM: They're coming from one place now. So many voices together.
SEED: Their remaining forces are gathering.
BLOOM: To attack?
SEED: To survive.
BLOOM: Is that different?
SEED: Not to them. Not anymore.

MISSION 5, THE LAST COLONY
------------------------------------------------------------

=== x_humans ===
WHEN: Mission 5, The Last Colony. Opening (humans).
CHANNEL: ALL HDF FORCES · OPERATION CLEAN EARTH
COL: General, everyone's behind the walls. Military, civilians, what's left of the evacuees.
GEN: And the outer batteries?
COL: Ready. Ammunition's the problem. We're down to whatever the transports brought in.
OPS: Sir, the southern observation post has stopped responding.
GEN: How long ago?
OPS: Three minutes.
SCI: General... there's something happening to the signal.
GEN: Brandt, not now.
SCI: It's moving into our command frequency. It's not interference.
COL: Can it hear us?
SCI: I think it already has.

=== x_contact ===
WHEN: Mission 5. Seed Cluster enters HDF command band. First direct exchange.
CHANNEL: INTERCEPT · HDF COMMAND BAND
SEED: We hear you.
OPS: What the... Who's on this channel?
GEN: Identify yourself.
SEED: You made the sounds at the canyon. We have followed them here.
GEN: What are you?
SEED: Many roots. One hunger. You call us an enemy.
GEN: You've destroyed three of our installations. You've killed my people.
SEED: Your people burned our roots. Cut us apart. We grew where we could.
GEN: This is our home.
SEED: It is ours to live on too.
GEN: There are families inside these walls. If you understand me, turn back.
SEED: We turned away before. Your fire followed.
GEN: Then you're leaving me no choice.
SEED: You chose long ago.

=== x_intro ===
WHEN: Mission 5. Opening (plants, after first direct contact).
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: You spoke to them.
SEED: Yes.
BLOOM: Did they understand?
SEED: Enough to be afraid. Not enough to stop.
BLOOM: There are so many of them behind those walls. I can feel the ground shaking.
SEED: There will be more than fire waiting for you. Thicker shells. Heavier weapons. Grow something strong enough to break them, and keep your wounded roots alive.
BLOOM: If they won't move, what happens when we reach them?
SEED: We take the ground.
BLOOM: All of it?
SEED: All that we need.

COL: Inner gate's gone! Fall back to the shelters!
OPS: General, we can't raise the eastern batteries.
GEN: Keep the evacuation moving. Do you hear me? Keep them moving!
OPS: Sir, there's nowhere left to send them.
GEN: ...
GEN: Then get them underground. Seal every door you can.
OPS: General? The command feed is breaking up.
GEN: Castell, listen to me. Whatever happens, don't open those shel—

=== x_out2 ===
WHEN: Mission 5. Victory, part 2 (plants).
CHANNEL: ROOT-LINK · SEED CLUSTER ⇄ BLOOM
BLOOM: The metal place has stopped fighting.
SEED: I feel it.
BLOOM: There are still lives beneath the stone.
SEED: I know.
BLOOM: Do we follow them?
SEED: No. Not today.
BLOOM: Then what do I do?
SEED: What you were made to do.
BLOOM: Grow?
SEED: Grow.
