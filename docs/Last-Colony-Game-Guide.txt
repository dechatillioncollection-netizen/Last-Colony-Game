Last Colony: Game Guide
=======================

October 15, 2026

Game overview

Last Colony is a top-down 2D factory-defence strategy game in the spirit of Mindustry. You play the invader for once. The Nymbri Hive is a living swarm of aquatic plant life that takes root on the planet Nereth, and the Human Defence Force (HDF) is fighting to stop it.

The factions

- Nymbri Hive: a single organism spread across many bodies. The Seed Cluster is its collective consciousness, the shared mind of the whole hive rather than a commander. On each map it plants a Bloom, a giant waterlily-like heart that stores everything the swarm gathers and spits at anything close. If the Bloom dies, you lose the map.
- Human Defence Force: the military of Nereth's human settlers, led by Supreme General Varrick. It fights with industrial machinery: drills, conveyors, steel walls, turrets, tanks, drones and bombers.

The species

- Common name: Nymbri. Scientific name: Nymbria communis.
- The name is a human invention and only provisional. The HDF's Biohazard Division gave it during the campaign. The genus is named after Dr Elena Nymbris, the Division's lead xenobiological researcher, and "communis" refers to the way the organisms seem to share functions and behaviour. The hive has no name for itself.
- Every Nymbri creature is a plant, including the ants. Parts of their tissue work like animal organs: they digest, move and sense.
- The Bloom and the Seed Cluster are two views of one mind, not a leader and a follower. The hive fights to survive, not out of hatred.

How you play

1. Grow Root Taps on ore veins (copper, lead, coal, sand, titanium) and lay Vines that carry the ore to your Bloom or straight into turrets.
2. Spread Rootweb, the purple living carpet your growths must stand on. It grows outward from the Bloom and from Rootweb Nodes.
3. Turn raw ore into better materials. Resin Glands digest coal into resin; Chitin Vats grow coal and sand into chitin.
4. Make vigor (power) with Sap Burners and Sunleaves to run advanced growths such as the Chitin Vat, Sunlance, Healing Pod and Brood Chamber.
5. Defend with spitters and bark walls fed by vine, then hatch ants and send them to Defend the Bloom or Attack.
6. Spend resources in the Evolution tree to unlock new growths.

Resources: Copper, Lead, Sand, Coal and Titanium are mined. Resin and Chitin are processed. Titanium needs a Deep Root.

Modes: a five-mission Campaign across the planet Nereth, plus two standalone modes, Survival and Base Siege. Optional extras are fog of war, the HD Remaster look, painted artwork for the Bloom and Spawning Pod, save slots and save files, and a Test Range save for trying units and turrets.

Nymbri Hive structures

The swarm has 26 growths, including the Bloom. All of them except the Bloom, Vines, Swift Vines and Vine Knots must be planted on Rootweb.

- Bloom (Heart, 3×3): The swarm's headquarters on each map. Stores everything gathered, grows Rootweb toward 30% of the map and spits at nearby enemies. Lose it and you lose the map.
- Thorn Spitter (Spitters, 1×1): Twin thorns at ground and air. Fed copper, resin or chitin.
- Spore Flak (Spitters, 2×2): Spore bursts against flyers only. Fed lead or chitin.
- Seed Mortar (Spitters, 1×1): Long-range exploding seed pods against ground targets. Fed resin or chitin.
- Sunlance (Spitters, 2×2): A piercing beam of light against ground targets. Uses 40 vigor.
- Root Tap (Roots, 2×2): Mines copper, lead, coal or sand and pushes it into adjacent vines.
- Deep Root (Roots, 2×2): A faster tap that can also break down titanium.
- Vine (Vines, 1×1): Carries items in the direction it grows. Units can walk over it.
- Swift Vine (Vines, 1×1): A vine about twice as fast. Grow it over old vines to upgrade them.
- Vine Knot (Vines, 1×1): Lets two vines cross without mixing.
- Splitter Bulb (Vines, 1×1): Splits incoming items evenly between its other sides.
- Sorting Gland (Vines, 1×1): Passes one chosen item straight through and sends the rest left or right.
- Resin Gland (Glands, 2×2): Digests 2 coal into 1 resin.
- Chitin Vat (Glands, 2×2): Grows 1 coal and 2 sand into chitin. Uses 30 vigor.
- Sap Burner (Vigor, 1×1): Burns coal for 60 vigor.
- Sunleaf (Vigor, 1×1): A trickle of 5 vigor from sunlight, no fuel.
- Rootweb Node (Rootweb, 1×1): Grows a new patch of Rootweb. Chain them outward.
- Spore Bellows (Rootweb, 2×2): Doubles Rootweb spread within 12 tiles. Uses 10 vigor.
- Bark Wall (Bark, 1×1): Basic wall that soaks up fire.
- Great Bark (Bark, 2×2): Four bark walls in one.
- Ironbark (Bark, 1×1): A much tougher wall hardened with titanium.
- Petal Gate (Bark, 1×1): A wall that opens for your ants. Too narrow for Bulwark Ants.
- Maw Gate (Bark, 2×2): A wide gate that opens for every ant, Bulwarks included.
- Healing Pod (Bark, 1×1): Mends your growths within 6 tiles. Uses 12 vigor.
- Spawning Pod (Brood, 2×2): Grows Mite Ants from copper and lead. No vigor needed.
- Brood Chamber (Brood, 3×3): Hatches Thornback and Bulwark Ants. Uses 30 vigor.

Evolutions that aren't buildings: Rapid Rooting makes Rootweb spread 60% faster, and Spore Winds adds another 60% and gives Rootweb Nodes 2 more tiles of reach.

HDF structures

The HDF builds 18 kinds of structure. It plays by the same rules as you: its drills and conveyors feed its turrets, factories and generators, and cutting that supply starves its defences.

- Colony Core (Command, 3×3): The colony's headquarters. Stores resources, pays for every unit it sends and fires at anything close. Destroy every core to win a base attack.
- Duo Turret (Turrets, 1×1): Twin-barrel gun that fires at your ants and growths. The colony's basic defence.
- Hail Cannon (Turrets, 1×1): Long-range artillery against ground targets.
- Flak Turret (Turrets, 2×2): Anti-air flak. The swarm has no flyers, so it never fires and works as a tough obstacle.
- Lancer (Turrets, 2×2): A charged energy beam against ground targets. Needs generator power.
- Mining Drill (Mining, 2×2): Mines copper, lead, coal or sand onto conveyors.
- Pneumatic Drill (Mining, 2×2): A faster drill that also mines titanium.
- Conveyor (Logistics, 1×1): Moves resources along a belt.
- Junction (Logistics, 1×1): Lets two conveyor lines cross.
- Router (Logistics, 1×1): Splits a conveyor line between its outputs.
- Graphite Press (Industry, 2×2): Presses coal into graphite, the colony's heavy ammunition.
- Silicon Smelter (Industry, 2×2): Smelts coal and sand into silicon. Needs power.
- Generator (Power, 1×1): Burns coal for power.
- Steel Wall (Defence, 1×1): Basic armour plating.
- Large Steel Wall (Defence, 2×2): Four steel walls in one.
- Titanium Wall (Defence, 1×1): Heavier titanium armour.
- Blast Door (Defence, 1×1): A door in the wall that opens for HDF units.
- Heavy Blast Door (Defence, 2×2): A wide door for heavy HDF vehicles.

The Test Range also shows HDF versions of the Titanium Conveyor, Sorter, Solar Panel, Mender and Ground Factory, but the colony never builds them in a match.

Units

Ants hatch from your Spawning Pods and Brood Chambers. HDF machines arrive in waves, or are paid for by a colony core in base attacks.

- Mite Ant (Nymbri Hive, hits ground): Small and cheap, grown from copper and lead. Weak alone, dangerous in packs.
- Thornback Ant (Nymbri Hive, hits ground and air): Quick warrior that spits thorns. Grown from chitin and lead.
- Bulwark Ant (Nymbri Hive, hits ground): Huge armoured ant that lobs acid seeds with splash. Grown from chitin, resin and titanium.
- Scout Tank (HDF, hits ground): Light tank and the backbone of every wave.
- Rammer Drone (HDF, hits ground): Fast drone that rams a target and explodes.
- Flame Tank (HDF, hits ground): Slow, tough tank with a short-range flamethrower.
- Siege Tank (HDF, hits ground): Heavy artillery tank that outranges most defences.
- Interceptor (HDF, hits ground (flies)): Fast aircraft that circles and strafes.
- Bomber (HDF, hits ground (flies)): Flies over walls and bombs your growths.

You order your swarm with one button: Defend keeps the ants near the Bloom, and Attack sends them after the enemy. You can also select ants and send them to a spot.

Campaign synopsis

The campaign is five sectors on the planet Nereth, played in order from a spinning globe. It traces first contact through to the fall of the last human colony. In Missions 1 to 3 each side only talks to itself, and the Seed Cluster speaks to the humans directly for the first time in Mission 5.

How the campaign works: each captured sector keeps producing resources into a shared global stock. Evolutions are paid from that stock and kept for every later sector. You can return to a captured sector with your base still running, for example to mine titanium once you have Deep Roots.

Rootweb in the campaign: every sector has a fixed map. The Bloom's own Rootweb only grows within its reach (11 tiles in Landfall, up to 18 in The Last Colony), which always covers the copper, lead and sand you need first. Plant Rootweb Nodes to carry the Rootweb further, toward the bigger coal and titanium fields. With a build tool selected, a dashed ring shows the Bloom's reach and each node's radius. Short radio messages from the HDF and remarks from the hive appear in the message feed as the waves go on; they never pause the game. Survival and Base Siege have no reach limit.

1. Landfall: hold 10 waves

A quiet meadow where the swarm first takes root. The Bloom wakes and tells the Seed Cluster what lies beneath it. The Seed Cluster tells it to feed itself and grow defences, though it doesn't yet know what from.

A mandatory tutorial teaches the controls: selecting units, moving the map, using structures, mining and building, then the first Thorn Spitter.

The map: a green meadow. The Bloom grows in a hollow on the west side, hidden from a dirt survey track that runs in from the east past survey markers, a parked survey truck and a radio mast. HDF waves follow that track.

Then comes a scripted first contact with combat switched off. HDF Patrol 7 (Private Kessler and Sergeant Okafor) walks up the track and finds purple growth that wasn't there yesterday. They see a spiked structure turn toward them and back away without firing. Okafor reports to Lieutenant Castell at HDF Command. Castell escalates to Supreme General Varrick and mentions that Dr Nymbris of the Biohazard Division wants samples, because the footage isn't enough to identify the growth. Varrick orders a cordon, samples for the doctor and burning. The patrol withdraws.

The Bloom tells the Seed Cluster that the hard things are gone but one reached toward it, and it reached back. Waves begin. After victory, the Seed Cluster concludes the humans were tearing their roots out, not feeding on them, and sends the Bloom north-east along the trails.

2. Ember Fields: hold 15 waves

Scorched coal plains, with flame tanks and bombers late on. You learn to digest coal into resin, a stronger thorn ammunition.

The map: ash plains with coal fields, rock ridges and burned ground. The HDF's Ember Depot stands fenced off in the north-east corner, and its haul road runs south-west past an abandoned drilling rig and excavator. Waves come down that road.

Before the fighting, the Biohazard Division gives HDF Command its preliminary assessment. This is where the species is named: Dr Nymbris tells Varrick the provisional classification is Nymbria communis, "Nymbri, for short". She explains that the tissue is mostly plant but works like animal organs in places, that the organisms seem to coordinate, and that nobody yet knows whether they are responding to threats or looking for them.

At the Ember Depot, Corporal Adeyemi teases Okafor about being chased off by a flower. Okafor admits the stories are true: it grows fast, hates fire and the spiky ones shoot back. Command warns the depot that Nymbri growth is moving toward the coal fields, and Okafor explains that's what the lab calls them now.

After victory, the Seed Cluster notices the pulses in the air change before each attack. One calls and many come, and the Bloom is told to find the caller.

3. Outpost Kilo: destroy the outpost

The first base attack: an HDF mining outpost guarding the only pass through the Kilo ridge. Its drills feed its guns, so you cut its belts, outrange it with Seed Mortars and send in Mite Ants.

The map: the Kilo ridge cuts the valley in two with a single pass. A road runs from the pass to the outpost, whose drills, belts and turrets sit outside its walls. A containment laboratory stands behind the core.

Varrick learns Ember Depot has fallen. Dr Nymbris reports that the Landfall samples keep growing in containment and respond to each other across separate containers, and that the ants' tissue matches the growths. She suspects the Rootweb carries signals between them. She also points out that Ember had been burning Nymbri growth for a week before it was attacked, which Varrick doesn't want to hear. Colonel Ruiz is ordered to hold the pass. The Bloom follows the ore into the walls, as the Seed Cluster instructed: take away what keeps their weapons working.

After victory, the Bloom finds pieces of the swarm sealed away, cut open and kept alive in the outpost's labs. The Seed Cluster realises the humans are learning about them, too.

4. Frost Canyon: hold 18 waves

A frozen canyon with titanium in its walls. Heavy siege tanks advance along the canyon floor with bomber wings overhead. You need Spore Flak, Sap Burners for vigor, Chitin Vats and Deep Roots.

The map: a frozen canyon between cliff walls with a frozen lake in the middle. The HDF stages artillery and tents at the head of the canyon road in the north and has put barricades across the canyon. Titanium lies at the foot of the cliffs.

Dr Nymbris shows Varrick that at Kilo the swarm cut the supply belts first, then the ammunition, then went for the command centre. That is planning, not just growth. She can't call them weeds anymore, and she notes that every assault logged so far came after a human one. Varrick refuses to wait. Varrick broadcasts a warning on every frequency: withdraw from our settlements or be destroyed. The Seed Cluster partly decodes it (boundaries, going away, destruction) and decides not to answer while it understands so little.

After victory, the human signals all come from one place. Their last forces are gathering to survive.

5. The Last Colony: destroy the fortress

A full mirrored fortress with walls, blast doors, Lancers and a working economy. Varrick makes a final stand here, with soldiers and evacuated civilians behind the walls.

The map: the valley is mirrored, so the colony's defences and economy match your side. Behind its core is a town with streets, 40 houses, HDF Command, a landing pad and three underground shelters. First contact plays from HDF Command, and the ending shows the shelters.

The Seed Cluster breaks into the HDF command band, and the two sides speak for the first time. It calls itself "many roots, one hunger" and says the humans call it their enemy. It never uses the name the humans gave it. Varrick accuses it of killing HDF soldiers. The Seed Cluster answers that the humans burned and cut the swarm first. Varrick begs it to turn back for the families inside. The Seed Cluster replies that it turned away before and the fire followed, and that the humans chose long ago.

To win you need Bulwark Ants, Thornbacks and Healing Pods.

In the ending, the inner gate falls. Varrick orders the survivors into sealed underground shelters as the command feed breaks up mid-sentence. The Bloom senses lives still beneath the stone and asks whether to follow them. The Seed Cluster says "Not now" and tells it to do what they are for: grow. The Bloom asks why they grow, and the Seed Cluster admits it does not know yet.

Survival and Base Siege

Both modes start with every growth already evolved and their own starting stock of 300 copper and 100 lead. Nothing in them touches your campaign stock or evolutions.

- Survival: a random 96×96 map. Hold your Bloom against 25 waves of HDF war machines that enter from a red zone. Waves grow from Scout Tanks to Rammer Drones, Interceptors, Flame Tanks, Bombers and Siege Tanks, with a boss every 10th wave.
- Base Siege: a large mirrored map with a full HDF colony on the far side. The colony mines, builds and pays for every unit it sends, so cutting its drills and conveyors weakens its attacks. You win by destroying its core.
