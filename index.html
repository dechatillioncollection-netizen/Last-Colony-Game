LAST COLONY

Root, spread, evolve, swarm.

  • You are the Brood, a plant-like alien ant swarm. You can only grow on Rootweb, the living ground that spreads out from your Bloom.
  • Your Bloom keeps spreading Rootweb on its own until it covers 30% of the map. Plant Rootweb Nodes at the edge to push it further. Evolve Rapid Rooting and Spore Winds, and grow Spore Bellows, to make it spread faster.
  • Put Root Taps on ore and run Vines to your Bloom and your Thorn Spitters. Open Evolution to unlock glands, vats, vigor and the Brood Chamber, which hatches warrior ants you can send to Defend or Attack.

Left-click grow or select · drag to lay a vine · R rotate · Right-click or drag to reabsorb · Q copy · T evolution · C swarm orders · WASD pan · wheel zooms · Space pause.
