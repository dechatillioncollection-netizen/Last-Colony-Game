LAST COLONY

Root, spread, evolve, swarm.

  • You are the Nymbri Hive, an alien species of plants that move, gather and fight like an ant colony. You can only grow on Rootweb, the living ground that spreads out from your Bloom.
  • Your Bloom keeps spreading Rootweb on its own until it covers 30% of the map. Plant Rootweb Nodes at the edge to push it further. Evolve Rapid Rooting and Spore Winds, and grow Spore Bellows, to make it spread faster.
  • Put Root Taps on ore and run Vines to your Bloom and your Thorn Spitters. Open Evolution to unlock glands, vats, vigor and the Brood Chamber, which hatches warrior ants you can send to Defend or Attack.

Left-click grow or select · drag to lay a vine (it routes round obstacles; Ctrl+drag steps it diagonally; Esc cancels the drag), or with nothing chosen to box-select ants · hold Alt to see buildable ground · F4 enemy route view · R or the wheel rotates a Vine before you place it (during a drag, the whole line) · Right-click or drag to reabsorb · Q copy · T evolution · C swarm orders · WASD pan · wheel zooms (with no Vine chosen) · Space pause.
